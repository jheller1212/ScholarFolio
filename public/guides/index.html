Narrative CV guides for grant applications

Funders increasingly replace publication lists and h-indices with narrative and evidence-based CVs. These guides summarise what each format asks for, link to the official documents, and show how to turn your Google Scholar profile into a first draft.

  • NWO Veni, Vidi, Vici narrative CV: how to write the evidence-based CVAcademic profile, 10 key outputs, word limits per instrument, and the full list of banned indicators.
  • ERC CV and Track Record: how to write the 4-page sectionUp to four pages, three sections, up to ten research outputs with a short explanation of each.
  • MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship CV: how to write section 4 of Part B-2Indicative length 5 pages, exact dates, and outputs described by their significance rather than by impact factor.
  • UKRI Résumé for Research and Innovation (R4RI): how to write the narrative CVFour modules plus optional context, one résumé for the whole team, word count set by each funding opportunity.
  • SNSF CV format: how to write your major achievementsFive elements, 1–3 narrative achievements, up to 10 selected works, and no proxy metrics.
  • DFG CV template (form 53.200): how to fill it inMandatory for all DFG programmes: four pages, up to 10 + 10 publications with a note on your role.

ScholarFolio exports editable Word drafts in NWO, ERC and MSCA formats from your public profile.
